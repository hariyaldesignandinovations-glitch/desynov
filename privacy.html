Who we are

DESYNOV is provided by Sohil Jain (“we”, “us”). For privacy questions, contact sohiljain16@icloud.com.

The essentials

DESYNOV does not include advertising or cross-app advertising tracking. We do not sell personal information. Creative documents are stored on your device, with optional private iCloud sync for supported content. A separate service checks eligibility for the seven-day trial.

Your creative work

Sketches, journals, moodboards, reference photos, voice notes, plans, room captures and other project files are created and stored locally. The trial service does not receive this content. DESYNOV does not upload your creative work to us to train AI models.

If you turn on iCloud Sync, supported journals, journal templates and moodboards are sent to your private iCloud storage under your Apple Account. Other project types are not covered by this sync. We do not have access to browse your private iCloud documents. Your device backup settings may also affect how Apple backs up local app data.

Exporting or sharing sends the files you choose to the destination you select. Those copies are then controlled by you and that destination. Exported content may include text, photos or location labels you added.

Optional Apple sign-in

If you choose Sign in with Apple, DESYNOV stores your Apple sign-in identifier and the name and email Apple provides in this device’s Keychain. This profile is not sent to the trial service. Apple sign-in is optional and is separate from the Apple Account used for iCloud or App Store purchases. Email/password and phone-number sign-in are not offered by the current app.

Optional passkey sign-in

If you add a passkey, the app sends a fresh Apple identity proof to our separate Cloudflare-hosted account service to confirm the Apple identity already signed in to DESYNOV. This proof can include an email address supplied by Apple; the service verifies it in memory and does not retain or log the proof, name or email. It stores your Apple sign-in identifier, a random account identifier, public passkey credentials, verification counters and hashed session tokens. These records let a passkey sign you into the same DESYNOV account. Your private passkey stays with your chosen credential provider; DESYNOV does not receive your biometric template or device passcode. Your credential provider controls passkey backup and synchronization. The account service does not receive creative documents.

When you request removal of Apple sign-in access, the app asks you to confirm with Apple. It sends a fresh identity proof and authorization code to the account service, which exchanges the code with Apple and requests token revocation. These codes and tokens are processed only for that request and are not retained or logged. This removal flow is also available for a local Apple sign-in profile without creating a passkey account.

Trial eligibility and Apple billing

When you choose “Start my 7-day trial” after reading the trial information, the app sends a randomly generated claim identifier and an Apple DeviceCheck token over HTTPS to our service hosted by Cloudflare. The service checks with Apple whether the device has already used its trial.

We retain the claim identifier, a signed trial record containing its start and expiry times, and issuance status. These records are linked to the device’s trial, not to your creative work or Apple sign-in profile. They let us recover the original trial after an interrupted request and prevent repeat trials. Raw DeviceCheck tokens are processed for the check and are not saved in our trial database or application logs.

Apple maintains the device’s trial-used flag. A device-only Keychain record also remembers trial eligibility locally. Removing the app, signing out or deleting creative files does not renew eligibility.

Subscriptions are purchased through Apple. The app reads verified subscription status to unlock access. We do not receive your payment-card or bank details, and the trial service does not receive purchase receipts. Apple processes payments and retains purchase records under its own policies.

Permissions and connected features

  • Camera and photos: used when you capture, import or export images, or scan a room. DESYNOV works with the photos you select and the permissions you grant.
  • Microphone and speech: used for voice notes, dictation and voice search. DESYNOV requests on-device speech recognition; supported languages and availability depend on your device.
  • Location: optional location access and photo metadata can provide a place label for journal photos. Apple’s mapping service may process coordinates to resolve that label. DESYNOV does not send them to the trial service.
  • Face ID or Touch ID: Apple’s authentication system can unlock the app or protected books. DESYNOV receives the authentication result, not your biometric template.
  • Motion: used locally for supported visual effects.

Built-in image analysis and other bundled machine-learning features process content on your device. When you explicitly open Apple’s Image Playground, selected prompts and reference images are provided to that system feature. Processing may use Apple services or other options you select in Apple’s interface; the applicable system disclosures and choices apply.

If you browse or download additional materials, the app requests catalog entries and files from ambientCG and its delivery hosts. Those providers receive the requested resource and normal network information such as your IP address. Your project images are not uploaded as part of these downloads. Opening an external reference link contacts that website.

Apple services are described in Apple’s privacy information. Cloudflare describes its platform processing in its privacy policy. Material-provider information is available from ambientCG.

Support messages and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any files you choose to attach. We use them to respond, troubleshoot and handle privacy requests. Please send only the information needed for your request; private drawings, passwords and full payment details are not required.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We have added no analytics scripts, advertising, tracking cookies or contact-form database. GitHub may process visitor IP addresses and normal request information to deliver and secure the site, as explained in GitHub’s privacy statement. Following links to other sites brings their privacy practices into play.

Retention, security and sharing

Local work remains until you delete it or remove the app’s stored data; exported files, device backups and iCloud copies have separate lifecycles. Turning sync off does not delete existing cloud copies.

Trial records currently have no automatic expiry-based deletion. We retain the limited eligibility and recovery record while operating the one-trial-per-device service, including after a trial ends or the app is removed. Apple’s device flag and local trial Keychain record also persist independently of creative-file deletion. Privacy requests are reviewed subject to applicable legal requirements and legitimate fraud-prevention needs.

Passkey account identifiers and public credentials remain until you delete the passkey account. Sessions expire after 30 days; sign-out revokes the current session when the service can be reached. Challenges expire after three minutes, abandoned new accounts after ten minutes, and hashed network rate-limit records after two minutes. Account deletion removes its active records and sessions immediately; a hashed anti-replay marker remains for up to ten minutes. Expired temporary records are removed by service cleanup. Cloudflare platform backups and operational retention are subject to its policies.

Support correspondence is kept for resolving the request and necessary follow-up, disputes or legal obligations. Hosting and email providers apply their own operational retention policies. Cloudflare may process network and security metadata even though our Worker’s application logging is disabled.

We use service providers as described above and may disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect the service and its users. No creative-content access is required for trial verification. We use HTTPS for the trial service and keep its signing credentials server-side; no system can guarantee absolute security.

Providers may process information in countries other than yours. Where applicable law requires it, relevant transfer protections apply. Where a legal basis is required, we rely on providing the requested service, legitimate interests in preventing abuse and responding to support, consent for optional processing where required, and legal obligations.

Your choices and data removal

  • Permissions: change DESYNOV’s camera, photo, microphone, speech and location access in iOS or iPadOS Settings.
  • Local work: export what you need, then use the relevant library’s deletion controls. Where Recently Deleted is available, a separate recovery or permanent-removal step may apply.
  • iCloud copies: open DESYNOV’s Account & iCloud settings and choose “Delete DESYNOV iCloud data”. This removes supported cloud copies while keeping local work. Turn sync off on your other devices first so their copies do not upload again. Wait for the app to confirm deletion or complete a pending retry.
  • Apple sign-in: in Account & iCloud, open “Remove account from this device”. Choose “Remove and revoke Apple access” to confirm with Apple and remove DESYNOV’s Apple authorization, or “Remove local profile only” to remove just the profile on this device. If revocation cannot complete, the app provides Apple’s removal instructions. These actions do not delete your Apple Account, creative work or subscription. Removing the local profile does not delete a server passkey account.
  • Passkeys: in Account & iCloud, choose “Delete passkey account” and confirm with a passkey to delete your server account and all its public credential records and sessions. “Delete and revoke Apple access” also asks for Apple confirmation and requests removal of DESYNOV’s Apple authorization. “Delete account only” remains available. If Apple revocation fails after server deletion, the account stays deleted and the app explains how to remove Apple access manually. Remove saved passkeys separately from your credential provider, such as Apple’s Passwords app. Removing only the local profile, deleting the app or removing a saved passkey does not delete the server account. If you cannot use a passkey, contact us for a verified deletion request.
  • Subscriptions: manage or cancel through Apple. Deleting the app, profile or documents does not cancel a subscription.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive your personal information, object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a data-protection authority. Contact us to exercise applicable rights. We may need enough information to verify the request; we cannot identify a device-only trial record using an email address alone. A data-removal request does not automatically grant another free trial.

DESYNOV is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us.
