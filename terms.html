App licence

DESYNOV is provided by Hariyal Design and Innovations Pvt Ltd. The App Store app is licensed under Apple’s Standard End User License Agreement. This page explains DESYNOV’s access and subscription arrangements; it does not replace that licence or remove rights you have under applicable consumer law.

Seven days to explore

A device eligible for its first DESYNOV trial receives seven days of app access from successful activation. Internet access and Apple’s device eligibility check are required. Each device is eligible once; reinstalling, signing out or removing local data does not provide another trial.

The app trial is not an automatically renewing subscription and does not charge you when it ends. To continue, you must choose a plan and confirm the purchase through Apple. If you subscribe before the app trial expires, the subscription is billed when you confirm; the remaining trial time does not postpone the purchase.

Monthly or three months

India reference prices

₹149 per month · ₹399 every three months.
Both plans unlock the whole app. There is no annual plan.

The App Store displays the current price, currency and billing period for your storefront before purchase. Prices in other currencies are Apple’s storefront prices and need not be a direct exchange-rate conversion.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends; renewal is charged within the 24 hours before that period ends. Manage or cancel through your Apple Account’s subscription settings. Access normally continues until the paid period expires, subject to refunds, revocation and Apple’s billing status.

Use “Restore Purchases” in DESYNOV if an existing active subscription is not recognised. For cancellation, see Apple’s instructions. Refund requests are handled through Apple’s refund process, with applicable consumer rights preserved.

Your work and access

You retain ownership of the creative work you bring to or create in DESYNOV, subject to any rights in third-party material you use. Keep separate exports of important files. Ending a trial or subscription does not itself delete saved work, but access to the app’s creative workspaces requires an active trial or subscription.

Feature availability depends on your device, operating system and relevant Apple services. Scans, dimensions, estimates and AI-generated results are design aids; check their accuracy and suitability before relying on them for construction, purchasing or other professional decisions.

How the app handles your information is explained in the Privacy Policy.
