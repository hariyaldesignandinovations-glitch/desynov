Common questions

How does the seven-day trial work?

Your first eligible device activation starts seven days of app access. Internet access is needed to verify eligibility. The trial does not automatically charge you. When it ends, a subscription through Apple is required to continue using the app. Reinstalling or removing your profile does not start a second trial on that device.

Which subscription plans are available?

Monthly and three-month plans unlock the whole app. India reference prices are ₹149 per month and ₹399 every three months. Apple displays the current price for your storefront and currency before you confirm. There is no annual plan. See subscription details.

I subscribed, but the app is still locked.

Check that the device uses the Apple Account that made the purchase, connect to the internet, and choose “Restore Purchases” on DESYNOV’s subscription screen. If access still does not return, contact support with the message shown. You do not need to send your Apple password.

How do I cancel or request a refund?

On iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions and DESYNOV. Follow Apple’s cancellation instructions. For a refund request, use Apple’s refund process; eligibility is determined by Apple and applicable consumer law. Deleting the app does not cancel billing.

Where is my work saved?

Project files are stored on your device. Optional iCloud Sync covers supported journals, templates and moodboards, not every project type. Use the export options available in each workspace for separate copies of important work. A trial or subscription ending does not itself delete saved documents, but app access is locked until you have an active subscription.

A sketch, import or render is not working.

Note the tool, file type and exact steps involved. Check that your device has free storage and the permissions required by that feature. For camera, LiDAR, Pencil or Apple Intelligence features, include the device model because support varies by hardware. Export important work before troubleshooting; do not delete and reinstall the app as a first step.

My iCloud work is not appearing on another device.

Confirm both devices use the same Apple Account in device Settings and have DESYNOV iCloud Sync enabled. Check the app’s sync status, internet connection and available iCloud space. The optional DESYNOV Sign in with Apple profile does not switch the iCloud account. Contact us if a pending or error status continues.

How do I remove my data or Apple sign-in profile?

Use the account and library controls described in Privacy choices and data removal. Local work, iCloud copies, the Apple sign-in profile, trial eligibility and subscriptions are separate. You can also email us with a privacy request.
